About the Chapter

South Central PA Chapter of Volleyball Officials

Our chapter is dedicated to developing knowledgeable, confident and professional volleyball officials. We support officials serving NCAA Division I, Division II and Division III competition and provide ongoing education, training and rating opportunities.
